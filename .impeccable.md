# Impeccable design context

Read this file together with DESIGN.md. DESIGN.md is the detailed, authoritative contract; this file
is the short context an implementation or design agent should keep in working memory. The live Home route
at http://localhost:5173/app is the source of truth for the current visual language. When a new screen
conflicts with Home, fix the screen or make a deliberate documented change to both files.

## Product

Cookfully is a self-hosted cooking and meal-planning tool with quiet nutritional intelligence. It helps
home cooks and households discover, save, understand, plan, shop, prep, and cook food. The audience is
broad: dietary needs, health goals, meal preparation, household organization, or simply better dinners.
Do not assume gym culture, weight loss, body composition, or macro optimization is the user's identity.

The product should feel like a beautiful personal kitchen made useful:

- warm, assured, appetizing, calm, capable, and quietly intelligent;
- food and cooking context in the foreground;
- numbers and provenance close at hand but never the personality;
- self-hosted without looking like an admin panel;
- modern through clear interaction design, not animation for its own sake.

## Canonical Home composition

Home is a living kitchen dashboard, not Recipes Lite. Its scan order is:

1. **Kitchen intro:** "Your kitchen · Thursday", a dynamic "Good morning"/"Good afternoon"/"Good evening"
   greeting, one calm sentence, and unobtrusive desktop search. Never say "Good night".
2. **Tonight + This week:** a wide editorial food hero with one "Start cooking" action beside a compact
   seven-day card with real open/planned states and a "Next up" row.
3. **Use soon + Quick actions:** a pale-green pantry attention module beside a separator-led list of
   "Plan tonight", "Add a recipe", and "Add a grocery item".
4. **Cook next:** a featured recipe and two contextual recommendations. Every recommendation explains why
   it is here.
5. **Recently saved + Grocery:** a compact four-item image shelf and a dark herb grocery prompt.

This composition gives Home useful density without filling the screen with arbitrary cards. Preserve the
wide hero/compact week-card relationship and the desktop rail's quietness.

## Reusable visual language

- **Fonts:** Afacad Flux Variable for greetings, titles, recipe names, and display; Inclusive Sans Variable
  for body, controls, navigation, labels, and data.
- **Canvas:** warm ivory. Raised surfaces are near-white; quiet pantry attention is soft mint; primary
  anchors are deep herb; saffron is a restrained discovery accent; tomato belongs to food imagery.
- **Nutrition:** calories stay neutral; protein is blue, carbohydrates orange, fat golden yellow, and
  fiber plant green. A visible label always accompanies color. Missing facts are written honestly.
- **Geometry:** 4px rhythm; 10px controls; 18px surfaces; 22px media; 999px pills only for compact
  statuses/filters. Use occasional organic asymmetric hero/pantry/grocery corners (56–72px) with restraint.
- **Depth:** tonal surfaces and spacing first, one-pixel lines where grouping needs help, small shadows only
  for overlap. No nested cards, glassmorphism, decorative rings, or anonymous card grids.
- **Media:** food imagery leads every recipe surface. Missing images use the same framed, curved,
  ingredient/plate fallback art—not emoji or a generic gray rectangle.
- **Copy:** human, cooking-first, neutral, concise. Prefer "Cook next", "Use soon", "Recently saved",
  and "Grocery" over internal product jargon.

## Components to reuse

Start with existing shared patterns before writing local CSS:

- KitchenShell, RailNav, MobileNav, and route-aware active states;
- PageIntro, ModuleHeading, EditorialHero/TonightCard, and WeekCard;
- UseSoon, QuickActions, RecipeMedia, RecipeFallbackArt, RecipeCard/RecipeShelf;
- RecipeMetadata/NutritionRibbon, KitchenCompanion, EmptyState, Skeleton, and ErrorRecovery;
- CommandPalette, sheets, and dialogs with proper focus management.

Anywhere a recipe appears, show the useful facts available: estimated total time, servings, calories,
protein, carbs, and fat. Keep discovery values compact and rounded; detail surfaces may disclose exact
decimals, coverage, source, and corrections.

## Responsive and interaction bar

Desktop uses a fixed 112px warm-ivory rail with centered icons, visible short labels, mint active state,
and a small saffron dot. Content uses the remaining width with 48px desktop/28px tablet inset. Mobile
uses a compact top bar with an 18px wordmark and fixed 72px bottom nav with equal, aligned hit boxes for
Home, Recipes, Plan, Grocery, and More. Mobile shelves remain touch-scrollable but hide scrollbar chrome;
the partial next card and snap spacing provide the affordance. Mobile is redesigned for touch and cooking;
it is not a compressed desktop.

Use 160ms feedback, 220ms disclosure, and 280ms meaningful entrance motion with ease-out. Prefer opacity
and transform; never animate layout dimensions. Good motion explains cause and response (planning drops,
serving recalculation, shared recipe media, cooking-step progress). Respect reduced motion and do not use
page-entry transforms on narrow mobile.

## Design review checklist

Before shipping a screen, compare it with Home at desktop and 390×844:

- Is the primary object and next action obvious in one scan?
- Is the visual hierarchy food → action → supporting evidence?
- Are cards the same media shape, curve, and baseline as related surfaces?
- Are time, servings, calories, protein, carbs, and fat shown honestly where available?
- Are empty/loading/error/partial/stale/manual states useful and shaped like the destination?
- Are navigation icons, labels, focus states, safe areas, and 44px targets correct?
- Is the page dense enough to be useful without becoming a dashboard of rectangles?
- Does the implementation use DESIGN.md tokens instead of local raw colors or one-off geometry?
