# Impeccable Design Context

Grounded in the project's own `DESIGN.md` (authoritative, mandated by AGENTS.md). Design work must
follow this context; where this file and DESIGN.md disagree, DESIGN.md wins.

## Users

One self-hosted owner or small household running a gym-focused recipe and nutrition planner. They
track food as both fuel and craft: they want the rigor of a fitness tracker (honest, exact, correctable
nutrition) combined with the warmth of a premium cookbook. Accessed on evergreen desktop and mobile
browsers; 390x844 is the narrow acceptance viewport. Kitchen context favors dark surfaces.

## Brand Personality

- **Tone:** Modern / Tech-Humanist. Confident, precise, authoritative, inviting.
- **Three words:** precise, warm, data-forward.
- **Emotional goals:** calm over overwhelm (complex nutrition never feels like a spreadsheet), trust in
  the honesty of values, muscle memory for macro meaning.

## Aesthetic Direction

- **Theme:** Dark Mode First. Deep Slate-Charcoal `#0f141b` foundation; high contrast for data; no pure
  black or pure white; cool-tinted greys throughout.
- **Primary accent:** Electric Blue `#2c91ff` for high-intent actions and completion.
- **Inviolable Macro Palette (never change):** Protein `#2c91ff`, Carbs `#e5a433`, Fats `#8a919f`.
- **Typography:** Hanken Grotesk for headlines (800/700, -0.02/-0.01em), Public Sans for body,
  JetBrains Mono exclusively for numerical data. Self-hosted Variable faces.
- **Shape language:** Soft-modern; 12px interactive radius, 16px cards, full-round macro pills,
  rounded progress caps.
- **Elevation:** Tonal layering L0 (background) → L1 (cards, 1px `#FFFFFF10` border) → L2 (modals,
  soft 24px blur shadow). Glass (12px blur) reserved for sticky chrome.
- **Anti-references:** flat spreadsheet feel; garish accents; any alternative macro color mapping;
  side-stripe border accents; gradient text; decorative-only glassmorphism.

## Design Principles

1. **Macros are the brand.** The three macro colors are inviolable and reserved for protein/carbs/fat
   meaning; accents earn their rarity.
2. **Density is contextual.** High density for data tables and ingredient lists; low density for
   discovery and editorial content.
3. **Numbers are honest and legible.** JetBrains Mono for every number; monospaced alignment; explicit
   provenance, coverage, and state labels (never hide uncertainty).
4. **Darkness is calm.** Layered surfaces and soft shadows instead of flat panels; generous dark space
   so complex data never overwhelms.
5. **Preserve behavior and meaning.** Polish never weakens the nutrition-first contracts: original
   ingredient text, exact decimals, provenance, correction precedence, and explicit
   loading/empty/partial/estimated/manual/stale/failed states.
