# Impeccable Design Context

`DESIGN.md` is authoritative. Design work must use its tokens, component contracts, responsive rules,
content language, rejection list, and page acceptance checklist.

## Users

Cookfully serves home cooks and households who want to plan, cook, and eat with more care. That includes
people eating for general health, dietary requirements, balanced nutrition, weight change, performance,
or simple organization. Their identity is not presumed to be fitness, dieting, or macro tracking. They
use desktop for importing and planning, and mobile in the kitchen, grocery store, and throughout the day.

## Core jobs

- Find, save, and understand recipes without transcription work.
- See useful nutrition in cooking language without operating a spreadsheet.
- Build a realistic week, adjust servings, and generate a grocery list.
- Correct estimates and understand uncertainty without losing trust.
- Reach advanced or self-hosting controls when needed without exposing them during ordinary cooking.

## Brand personality

- **Direction:** editorial kitchen utility.
- **Three words:** warm, assured, appetizing.
- **Emotional outcome:** calm enough to start, confident enough to trust, and satisfying enough to return.
- **Memorable principle:** food is the foreground; nutrition is the quiet evidence layer beneath it.

## Non-negotiable design principles

1. Food before figures.
2. One clear next action per region.
3. Progressive disclosure before form density.
4. Neutral, non-moralizing health language.
5. Exactness and provenance remain available without polluting discovery surfaces.
6. Mobile is redesigned for cooking and touch, not shrunk from desktop.
7. shadcn may supply accessible primitives; Cookfully supplies the identity and compositions.

## Anti-references

Gym dashboards, dark technical control panels, identical card grids, generic wellness SaaS, default
shadcn demos, form walls, gradient-text heroes, decorative glass, and macros used as brand personality.
